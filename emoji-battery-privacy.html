Emoji Battery: DIY Widgets

Privacy Policy / Política de privacidad · 18 November 2026

English

Developer: LauraApps. The app reads your battery percentage and charging status on your device to display them in an Android home-screen widget and preview. Your selected theme is stored only in local app preferences. Version 1.6.0 does not collect, sell, share or transmit personal data. It does not request Internet, location, photos, files, contacts, microphone or camera access, and does not include ads or analytics SDKs. The optional Android screensaver displays battery status locally while Android activates it. Only after your explicit confirmation, the app can apply a static lock-screen wallpaper using Android's normal SET_WALLPAPER permission; the wallpaper does not update automatically. No accessibility, overlay or lock-screen bypass is used. No account is necessary. You can erase saved settings by clearing app storage or uninstalling it. Future versions with ads or analytics would require this policy and Google Play declarations to be updated before release.
